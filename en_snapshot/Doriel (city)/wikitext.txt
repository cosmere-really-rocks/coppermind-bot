'''Doriel''' is a city in the [[Elendel Basin]].{{map ref|Elendel Basin}} It is southeast of [[Elendel]].

It shares the name of the [[Ashmount]] Doriel, and is in fact built on the flats where that Ashmount used to be before the [[Catacendre]].{{map ref|Final Empire|the Final Empire}}{{rpg ref|mbwg|24}}

Doriel is the largest city in the Elendel Basin outside of Elendel itself that has a railway interchange.{{book ref|mb6|5}}{{rpg ref|mbwg|31}} The Doriel interchange has a branch that leads to [[Tarier]].{{map ref|Elendel Basin}}
