The '''Canton of Cartography''' is a division of the government in [[Elendel]]. They keep up to date maps of [[Elendel Basin]] and the city.{{map ref|Elendel}}{{map ref|Elendel Basin}} This particular Canton does not appear to have existed in the [[Final Empire]].

According to [[Nazh]], the Canton does not seem to care about the accuracy of its maps outside of Elendel. He was forced to annotate their Basin map to indicate the locations of [[Thermoli]] and the river and dam near [[Corbeau]].{{book ref|mb5}}
