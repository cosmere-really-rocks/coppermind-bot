'''Sliverism''' is a religion on [[Scadrial]] that arose after the [[Final Ascension]].

Sliverism is an evolution of the dominant religion of the [[Final Empire]], brought to the new world by [[Obligator|obligators]] such as [[Aradan Yomen]] that survived the Final Ascension. They seem to be looked down upon by many outsiders.{{book ref|mb4|4}} Sliverism incorporates many traditions and rituals from the [[Steel Ministry]] but actively distances itself from the more oppresive aspects of the Lord Ruler's government.{{rpg ref|mbwg|95}}

==Beliefs==

Sliverism teaches that the [[Lord Ruler]] was the Sliver of Infinity, an independent portion of an omnipotent and omniscient god more powerful than even the [[Shards]]. Sliverists believe that the Lord Ruler was a harsh and imperfect ruler who ultimately did what was right by defending humankind from [[Ruin]]. They also believe that Rashek fulfilled the [[Terris prophecies]] and was the Hero of Ages. Sliverist belief holds that when the Lord Ruler died his power splintered throughout the cosmere and came to be held by beings such as [[Marsh|Ironeyes]]. They believe that holding a portion of this divine power necessitates a right to rule. Sliverists believe that the greatest action a person can perform is service to the greater good.{{rpg ref|mbwg|95}}{{rpg ref|mbwg|96}}

==Practice==

Sliverists meet on the sixteenth day of every month in small chapels to listen to sermons and perform public services together. Four times a year Sliverists will also gather in [[Doriel (city)|Doriel]] or the [[Faleast Range]] at the sites of former [[ashmounts]] to bless the Lord Ruler in thanks for protecting humanity for a thousand years. Sliverists also celebrate All Slivers Night on the night between the months of Marsh and Doxin to commemorate the Lord Ruler's death. The holiday features dawn to dusk celebrations and the donning of [[Steel Inquisitor]] masks. All Slivers Night is popular even among non-sliverists.{{rpg ref|mbwg|96}}

The Sliverist priesthood is divided into three Orders based on old Steel Ministry Cantons. These Orders determine what the scholarly priests study and what services they perform to the community. The Order of Finance handles financial programs in their local community, the Order of Resources studies civil engineering and creates public works, and the Order of Orthodoxy studies philosophy and delivers sermons to followers. Rumors persist of a secretive Order of Inquisition that performs assassinations and other such unsavory works for the greater good of their communities. Such claims seem to be entirely unfounded. Sliverist priests wear high-collared black vestments with white trims. Sliverist priests will also mark particularly notable accomplishments within their Order with tattoos around their eyes.{{rpg ref|mbwg|96}}
