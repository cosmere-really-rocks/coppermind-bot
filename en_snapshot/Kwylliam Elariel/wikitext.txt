'''Kwylliam Elariel''' is a member of [[House Elariel]] in the fourth century after the [[Catacendre]]. He used House Elariel’s funds to found the [[Foundation Chronicle]].{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}} He belongs to a social circle called the [[Gilded Few]], a group of [[noble]] supremacists.

== Appearance and Personality ==
Kwylliam is a lanky man in his mid-twenties with defined cheekbones, golden-brown skin, and umber hair.{{rpg ref|mbl|14}} He wears extravagant fashion.

Kwylliam is provocative, and does and says whatever he pleases.{{rpg ref|mbl|14}} He is almost always sarcastic and nonchalant and hates boredom.

== Attributes and Abilities ==
Kwylliam is a [[Twinborn]], with access to [[Allomancy|Allomantic]] [[zinc]] and [[Feruchemy|Feruchemical]] [[duralumin]].{{rpg ref|mbl|14}} He also has access to Feruchemical [[chromium]] due to a [[bendalloy]] [[Hemalurgy|spike]].

== History ==
=== Order of a Thousand Eyes ===
Kwylliam was the eldest of four siblings, and received a top-class education and upbringing.{{rpg ref|mbl|14}} He spent most of his time focusing on debauchery, leisure, and petty crimes, which his family would bail him out of as needed.

He attended the [[University of Elendel]], where he became friends with [[Bayron]] and [[Lysarra]]. The three initiated into the [[Order of a Thousand Eyes]] fraternity and joined the [[Gilded Few]]. There they learned about the [[Eternity Spike]], and the three were able to trick [[NeBaal]] into revealing information about a [[Guilded Map|map]] leading to it.{{rpg ref|mbl|111}}

After graduating, he ran several broadsheets, including the ''The Truth in Elendel'', the ''[[Foundation Chronicle]]'', and the ''Truth Press''.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}{{rpg ref|mbl|14}}{{rpg ref|mbl|145}} He also became the Director of the National Archives for the University of Elendel.{{file ref|MBML The Elendel Daily broadsheet.png|''The Elendel Daily'' broadsheet}}

=== Lysarra's Celebration ===
Kwylliam bribed the National Archives to redirect a crate of artifacts meant for [[Azmine Wilko]] to the [[House Elariel|Elariel]] estate, which included the Raven Mask and a Founder's Key.{{rpg ref|mbl|92}}{{rpg ref|mbl|95}}{{rpg ref|mbl|107}} The day it was meant to arrive, he participated in a motorcar race, where the top three racers earned access to Lysarra’s birthday celebration at the National Archives.{{rpg ref|mbl|93}} During the party, the crate was stolen by Wilko's companions, who had previously entered the race.{{rpg ref|mbl|110}}

=== The Midnight Trials ===
Roughly a week later, Kwylliam officiated the Midnight Trials to select new members into the Order of a Thousand Eyes.{{rpg ref|mbl|111}} During the trials, the companions of Wilko discovered and attempted to rescued NeBaal from the Gilded Few.{{rpg ref|mbl|131}} Kwylliam confronted them, offering to duel them.{{rpg ref|mbl|132}} It is unknown if he won, but he allowed them to leave with the [[kandra]].{{rpg ref|mbl|133}}

=== Ascension ===
Weeks later, Kwylliam, Bayron, and Lysarra located the Eternity Spike and attempted to use it to have one of them become the next [[Lord Ruler]].{{rpg ref|mbl|182}} It is unknown which person they chose to Ascend, but the one chosen turned into a [[Hemalurgic monstrosity]] and was stopped by Wilko's companions.{{rpg ref|mbl|187}}
