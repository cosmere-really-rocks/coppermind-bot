'''Stevan Broche''' is the head chef of [[Monogram]] in [[Elendel]]. In the year {{date|342|PC}}, he devised the new spring menu, heavily featuring imported shrimp, crabs, oysters, crayfish and other exotic goods from [[New Tremredare]]. The menu was developed in partnership with [[Ashwinna Tekiel]]. The new menu was featured in an article in [[The House Record]].{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
