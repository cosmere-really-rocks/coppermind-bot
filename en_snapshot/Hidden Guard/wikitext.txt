The '''Hidden Guard''' is an elite paramilitary division of the [[Set]] that operates in the [[Elendel Basin]] on [[Scadrial]]. Their members wear red uniforms.{{book ref|mb6|29}}

== Structure and Function == 
The Hidden Guard serves as armed guards to high-ranking members of the Set, such as [[Telsin]] and [[Edwarn Ladrian]], and they also defend Set-owned assets and buildings.{{book ref|mb6|17}}{{book ref|mb6|27}} The overall size of the Hidden Guard is unclear, but at minimum there are hundreds of members.{{book ref|mb7|61}}

They wear red soldier's uniforms with an identifying mark on the breast.{{book ref|mb6|17}} They appear to have a structured chain of command{{book ref|mb6|17}} and are very respectful of the ranking members of the Set, referring to them formally and saluting their orders.{{book ref|mb6|27}}{{book ref|mb6|29}} They seem to be well-trained and disciplined, and are equipped with modern rifles and even [[aluminum]] weaponry.{{book ref|mb7|61}} The Hidden Guard is more than just infantry; some members are trained in other roles needed to ensure the safety of assets under their protection. They have scouts,{{book ref|mb7|61}} mechanics, and technicians who have knowledge of the [[Metallic Arts]].{{book ref|mb6|29}} 

The soldiers sometimes receive support from the Set's [[Allomancer]]s, but the Guard does not command them directly.{{book ref|mb6|29}}{{book ref|mb6|20}} A [[Feruchemist]] named [[Fed]] is noted as working with the Hidden Guard,{{book ref|mb6|29}} but it is unclear if she is actually a member. The Hidden Guard is the Set's most elite military unit, but it is not their only armed force. For example, the soldiers in [[Wayfarer]] do not appear to be part of the Hidden Guard{{book ref|mb7|51}} and the Set also hires mercenaries for tasks like low-level guard work.{{book ref|mb7|63}}

== History ==
[[Wax]] and [[Wayne]] clashed with the Hidden Guard on several occasions. 
=== Dulsing and the Sovereign's Temple ===
In {{date|342|PC}}, their soldiers were first observed by [[Wax]] at the Set's compound in [[Dulsing]] that was built around the wreck of the airship ''[[Brunstell]]''. There were a "ton" of soldiers in red stationed in and around the warehouse, patrolling it for any intruders. Many of them attempted to kill Wax and his allies once they were caught trying to infiltrate.{{book ref|mb6|17}}{{book ref|mb6|20}}

Telsin and Edwarn brought some members of the Hidden Guard with them on their expedition to the [[Sovereign's temple]].{{book ref|mb6|27}} They helped investigate the [[Southern_Scadrian#The_Hunters|Hunter]] airship that had crashed at the site years earlier.{{book ref|mb6|27}}{{book ref|mb6|29}} The Hidden Guard soldiers attempted to defend the site, but many of them were easily defeated when [[Marasi]] tapped into the [[Bands of Mourning]].{{book ref|mb6|28}} After the airship was inspected and prepared for flight, a Hidden Guard technician who was involved in the restoration of the ship gave Edwarn a [[Feruchemy#Unsealed_Metalminds|weight medallion]] so he could control the ship. After Edwarn ordered the ship to depart, the technician asked if they should be waiting for Telsin, but Edwarn told him that she would join them later if she could.{{book ref|mb6|29}}

=== Bilming ===
In {{date|348|PC}}, the Hidden Guard was under Telsin's command.{{book ref|mb7|33}} A large number of soldiers were stationed at [[Independence Tower]], the Set's headquarters in [[Bilming]]. When Wax and Wayne attacked the tower, they guards used aluminum guns and other weapons in an attempt to protect Telsin. Despite the Set's tactical advantages, Wax (referring to himself as "[[Harmony]]'s Sword") was able to defeat the soldiers while sustaining only minor injuries.{{book ref|mb7|61}}{{book ref|mb7|62}} Wayne believed that none of the soldiers were left after Wax unleashed his abilities.{{book ref|mb7|63}}
