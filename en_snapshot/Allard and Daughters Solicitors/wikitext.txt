'''Allard and Daughters Solicitors''' is a law firm in [[Elendel]]. During the year {{date|342|PC}}, they sued [[House Erikeller]] because the steel mills owned by that house allegedly had sub-standard safety procedures. As part of their lawsuit, they posted an advertisement in [[The House Record]] urging anyone with knowledge about the safety procedures in these mills to come forth and speak to them.{{rpg ref|cfm2|23}}
