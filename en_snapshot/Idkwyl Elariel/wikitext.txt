'''Idkwyl Elariel''' is a member of [[House Elariel]] in the fourth century after the [[Catacendre]]. She is an up-and-coming fashion designer in [[Bilming]] whose name suggests likely Terris heritage.{{file ref|The_Two_Seasons_broadsheet.jpg|''The Two Seasons'' broadsheet}}

In {{date|348|PC}}, Idkwyl made multiple [[Terris]]-inspired designs. These designs were sold in a flagship store located in [[Bilming]]. Her designs were advertised in the ''[[The Two Seasons]]'' broadsheet published on Marewill 19, {{date|348|PC}}.{{file ref|The_Two_Seasons_broadsheet.jpg|''The Two Seasons'' broadsheet}}
