'''Garmet''' is a city in the very southeastern portion of the [[Elendel Basin]]. It is adjacent to the [[Seran Range]] and southeast of [[Elmsdel]].{{map ref|Elendel Basin}} Garmet is a perfect example of [[Elendel]]’s economic policies. Because the railways and canals all go towards Elendel, stone quarried in Garmet has to travel five hundred miles before it reaches [[New Seran]], while the two are only one hundred miles apart overland.{{rpg ref|mbwg|66}}
