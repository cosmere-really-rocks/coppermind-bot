The '''Silver Voyager club''' is an establishment in [[Elendel]]. The [[Elendel Explorer's League]] often gathers at the Silver Voyager club. Due to this, the club seems to have a bad reputation. The Silver Voyager was mentioned in an edition of [[The House Record]] as being a disreputable club, whereas the [[Observatory]] on [[Stardial Row]] was a respectable establishment.{{rpg ref|cfm2|23}}
