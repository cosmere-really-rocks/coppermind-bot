'''New Tremredare''', also called the '''Festival City''', is a city in the [[Conrad Delta]] on [[Scadrial]]. It lies on the very edge of the delta, right on the coast of the [[Sea of Lennes]]. The city was founded by [[Ashwinna Tekiel]] sometime after the late 200s, but before the year {{date|343|PC}}. It has a little less than twenty thousand inhabitants.{{rpg ref|mbwg|189-191}} New Tremredare does a lot of trade with [[Elendel]], which imports oceanic foods such as shrimp, crabs, crayfish and oysters from New Tremredare.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}} Culturally and economically the region has great ties with [[New Seran]], with talks of a rail line being laid to connect the two.{{rpg ref|mbwg|189-191}}
