'''Survivorday''' is a post-[[Catacendre|Catacendric]] holiday celebrated in the [[Elendel Basin]] and the north of [[Scadrial]]. It is the holiest day for the [[Survivorism|Survivorist Church]], observed on the first of [[Kels]], the second month.{{rpg ref|mbwg|28}} Many people get the day off from work on Survivorday.{{file ref|The_Two_Seasons_broadsheet.jpg|''The Two Seasons'' broadsheet}}
