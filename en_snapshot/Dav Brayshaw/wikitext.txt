'''Dav Brayshaw''' is a farmer in [[Drypost]] on [[Scadrial]]. He is the eldest son of a large [[koloss]]-blooded family. One day in the year {{date|342|PC}}, Dav saw an unmistakable glint in his irrigation trenches. He traced the gold upstream and found quartz deposits in an exposed hillside. After a few days of digging, he uncovered a rich gold vein.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
