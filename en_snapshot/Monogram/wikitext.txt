'''Monogram''' is a restaurant in [[Elendel]]. In {{date|342|PC}} the head chef, [[Stevan Broche]], and [[Ashwinna Tekiel]] crafted a new spring menu. The new menu heavily featured imported shrimp, crayfish, crabs, oysters and other exotic goods from [[New Tremredare]]. The menu was featured in an article in [[The House Record]].{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
