The '''Conrad Delta''', named by its surveyor [[Avery Conrad]] in the late 200s PC, is a delta formed in the [[Southern Roughs]] where the runoff from the nearby mountains meets and empties into the [[Sea of Lennes]], covering more than 625 sq miles. It is inhabited by around twenty thousand people, most residing in [[New Tremredare]] founded by Lady [[Ashwinna Tekiel]]. The region is notable for the [[Kovlair]] and [[Dramal]] [[koloss]] clans that reside there and the numerous pre-[[Catacendre]] ruins that dot the area, including [[Tomb City]] overseen by [[Goldie Glitterhollow]]. Culturally and economically the region has ties with [[New Seran]], with talks of a rail line being laid to connect the two.{{rpg ref|mbwg|189-191}}
