The '''Day of Ascent''' is a post-[[Catacendre|Catacendric]] holiday celebrated during [[Vinuarch]] in the [[Elendel Basin]] and the north of [[Scadrial]]. It is a celebration of [[Vin]], the Ascendant Warrior. It likely takes place on the day that she Ascended.{{rpg ref|mbwg|28}}
