The '''Hot Tin Orchestra''' is a popular orchestra on [[Scadrial]]. The orchestra has a total of sixteen members, and the band’s leader is called [[Andromedine Hukainly]]. They are based in the 6th octant and often perform at the [[Gold Button Bar]] and [[Shywood Hall]]. Combining woodwinds, horns, drums, and a beat that really swings, the Hot Tin Orchestra is taking the 6th octant by storm. Some rumours state that the band has [[Rioter]]s in it.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
