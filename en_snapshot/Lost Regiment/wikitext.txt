The '''Lost Regiment''' was a regiment of [[skaa]] soldiers who rebelled against the [[Lord Ruler]] and fled to the [[Crescent Dominance]]. It is unknown when this occurred, or what happened to them. However, during the year {{date|342|PC}}, [[Matilda Fendreau]] was cleaning out her uncle’s attic and found a trunk with several defaced insignia which she traced back to the Lost Regiment.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
