The '''National Archives''' is a building in [[Elendel]] on [[Scadrial]].{{book ref|mb5|6}}

The National Archives are located in the Second Octant, near the [[Field of Rebirth]] and across from the governor's mansion.{{book ref|mb5|6}} The archives store important historical documents, such as documents from which the origins of [[Final Empire]] insignia can be traced. [[Matilda Fendreau]] went to the National Archives after she discovered several defaced Final Empire insignia in her uncle’s dusty attic. With help, she discovered that they had belonged to members of the [[Lost Regiment]].{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}

Governor [[Replar Innate]] liked to address people from the governor's mansion. On at least one such occasion, in {{date|342|PC}}, the constables kept an eye on the people from the steps leading up to the National Archives.{{book ref|mb5|6}}
