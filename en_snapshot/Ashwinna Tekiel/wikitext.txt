'''Ashwinna Tekiel''' is a member of [[House Tekiel]]. Sometime after the late 200s,{{rpg ref|mbwg|189-191}} but before the year {{date|343|PC}}, she founded the city of [[New Tremredare]] in the [[Conrad Delta]]. In {{date|342|PC}}, she partnered with [[Stevan Broche]] to create a new spring menu for [[Monogram]], heavily featuring goods from New Tremredare.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
