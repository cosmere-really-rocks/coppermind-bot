'''Foundation Chronicle''' is a publication in [[Elendel]]. It was founded somewhere before {{date|343|PC}} by [[Kwylliam Elariel]]. The publication is known among many people for its shoddy reporting, base gossip and tasteless sensationalism. It has encountered major troubles at least twice because of these issues. The publication was mentioned in an article of [[The House Record]]. The article talked about how Foundation Chronicle had been exposed for a second time and also featured a story by widower [[Hal Scaffen]].{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
