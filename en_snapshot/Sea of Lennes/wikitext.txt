The '''Sea of Lennes''' is a body of water adjacent to the [[Elendel Basin]] on [[Scadrial]]. It lies south of the [[Sea of Yomend]] and makes up the southwest coast of the Basin.{{map ref|Elendel Basin}} The sea is fed by water from the [[Conrad Delta]]. People from [[New Tremredare]] fish the sea for food, such as shrimp, crayfish, crabs and oysters.{{rpg ref|mbwg|189-191}}
