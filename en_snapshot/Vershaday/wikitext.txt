'''Vershaday''' is a post-[[Catacendre|Catacendric]] holiday celebrated during [[Tenson]] in the [[Elendel Basin]] and the north of [[Scadrial]]. It celebrates the [[koloss]] hero called [[Human]] and the heritage of koloss-blooded people.{{rpg ref|mbwg|28}}
