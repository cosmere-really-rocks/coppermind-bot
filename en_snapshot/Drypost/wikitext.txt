'''Drypost''' is a city in the [[Elendel Basin]]. It is far to the north and east of [[Elendel]], and lies on the railroad leading into the Northern [[Roughs]].{{map ref|Elendel Basin}} Drypost is ruled by a council of executives from mining and rail companies.{{rpg ref|mbwg|65}} In {{date|342|PC}}, [[Dav Brayshaw]], a farmer from Drypost, uncovered a rich gold vein on his land.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
