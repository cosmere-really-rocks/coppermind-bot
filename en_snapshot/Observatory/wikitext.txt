The '''Observatory''' is an establishment in [[Elendel]]. They are located on [[Stardial Row]], just across from [[Tekiel Park]]. They launched an advertisement in [[The House Record]], demeaning their rival, the [[Silver Voyager club]], and the [[Elendel Explorer's League]], which often gathers there. They called the EEL a 'loose affiliation of disenfranchised and disheveled nobility'. Instead, the advertisement suggests that 'gentlefolk of class' have a 'quiet' drink with 'respectable' people at the Observatory.{{rpg ref|cfm2|23}}
