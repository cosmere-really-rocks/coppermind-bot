'''Jeddifry Tekiel''' is a member of [[House Tekiel]]. He was a student at [[Elendel University]], where he tragically died in the year {{date|335|PC}}. Professor [[G. Geffenry]] later insinuated to [[The House Record]] that his accidental death was a result of actions by university clubs. There is no hard evidence for this however, and these allegations are mainly repeated by students without noble ancestry.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}
