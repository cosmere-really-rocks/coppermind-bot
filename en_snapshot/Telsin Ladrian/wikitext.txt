'''Telsin Ladrian''' is a member of House Ladrian on [[Scadrial]] and sister of [[Waxillium]].{{book ref|aol|3}} She is a high-ranking member of the [[Set]] who is referred to by underlings as Lady Sequence, and eventually rose to the position of the Key, the leader of the Set.{{book ref|mb6|27}}{{book ref|mb7|21}} Eventually, she became an [[avatar]] of [[Autonomy]].{{book ref|mb7|19}}

== Appearance and Personality ==
{{image|Telsin by Howuld.png|side=left|height=220px}}
Telsin is middle-aged and has dark hair in curls and a rugged body.{{book ref|mb6|19}} While living in the [[Village]] as a teen, she showed disdain for following rules.{{book ref|mb6|Prologue}} Waxillium describes her as duplicitous and manipulative, being good at hiding her true intentions and always being able to get what she wants.{{book ref|mb7|19}} She loved to draw, so her uncle kept an easel with a large pad of paper in [[Ladrian mansion]] for her use.{{book ref|aol|3}}

== Attributes and Abilities ==
Telsin is a [[Hemalurgy|Hemalurgist]], and originally had three spikes, granting her the power of a [[steel|Coinshot]], [[gold|Bloodmaker]], and [[chromium|Leecher]] respectively, due to the fact that having four or more Hemalurgic spikes would make her susceptible to Harmony's complete control.{{book ref|mb6|26}} After the Set learned how to bypass this restriction, she would exchange spikes as required, granting her the power of a [[iron|Lurcher]] and [[tin|Windwhisperer]].{{book ref|mb7|34}}{{book ref|mb7|35}}{{rpg ref|mbwg|147}}

As an avatar of Autonomy, she’s able to communicate with her followers and “visualize plans in greater complexity than an ordinary human."{{book ref|mb7|66}}

== History ==
=== Youth ===
Like her brother Wax, she lived in the Village for a time as a youth and was subject to their [[Edwarn Ladrian|uncle's]] attempts to wrest their upbringing from their parents. When Wax left the Village after killing [[Forch]], she did not leave at that time. While Wax felt like an outsider, Telsin integrated with her peers quite easily. She often attempted to sneak out to go drinking.{{book ref|mb6|prologue}} When she wanted her own room, she planted stolen cash on her cousin to get what she wanted. Not only did she get a private room- living alone made it much easier to sneak out.{{book ref|mb7|35}}

Telsin and Wax were not close; they were practically strangers.{{book ref|aol|3}} Over the course of twenty years with very little contact between them, they both fell prey to laxity in their attempts to stay in touch.

=== The Set ===
As a young adult, while she was mingling among the influential and powerful of Elendel, she discovered the Set.{{rpg ref|mbwg|147}} She joined the Set, recruiting her uncle Edwarn Ladrian to ensure she’d have the funds necessary for her prospects. Upon making contact with Trell, Telsin rapidly rose through the Set’s ranks. When Edwarn faked his own death in the carriage accident, he faked hers as well.{{book ref|mb6|35}}

Initially, she pretended to be a captive of the Set as a means to control her brother. When Wax confronts Edwarn, Edwarn tells Wax that if he kills him, "your sister is as good as dead." Wax promises to address her kidnapping after dealing with [[Bleeder]].{{book ref|mb5|17}}
{{image|Telsin Ladrian-MotP.png|side=right|height=250px}}

Wax finds Telsin under guard in a secret Set fortress in [[Dulsing]]. Wax rescues her, and she acts as if she wants revenge on all the members of the Set. When the Set's [[Thug]] enters the room, she immediately shoots him dead, blowing their cover and leading to a desperate escape on the skimmer.{{book ref|mb6|19}}

Telsin convinces Wax that they must go to the temple before Suit's expedition so that they can claim the [[Bands of Mourning]]. As they fly there, the skimmer flies over the group and Telsin "accidentally" causes [[Wayne]] to kick a pack overboard, hitting one of the guards and alerting them to their presence.{{book ref|mb6|23}} This action, along with her quick shooting of the Thug (who would have revealed her true identity), causes Wax to suspect her.

After encountering Suit and entering the temple, Telsin betrays Wax and shoots him three times before he can escape through a trap. She also incapacitates [[MeLaan]], injecting her and taking her spikes. She was a member of the Set all along. Known as Lady Sequence, she outranks even Suit.{{book ref|mb6|27}} 

After [[Marasi]] finds the Bands of Mourning and turns the tide of the encounter, Telsin attempts to escape on the skimmer. Wayne finds her and shoots her multiple times with a shotgun to avenge Wax's (temporary) death. He figures she has Hemalurgic health anyway, so he doesn't have much compunction about shooting her.{{book ref|mb6|29}} After reclaiming the spikes she was carrying, he lets her go.{{book ref|mb6|31}} Later, Wax finds the skimmer gone, indicating that she had escaped.{{book ref|mb6|30}}

=== Avatar of Autonomy ===
Autonomy believed that Scadrial was dangerous and needed to be destroyed. Telsin convinced her to spare the planet if she could prove she was capable of conquering the Basin. On this condition, Autonomy chose her to become her avatar.{{book ref|mb7|18}}

Telsin began building a “new empire” in Bilming in 342 PC. LM 35 There she developed the city socially and technologically as a staging ground for her plans.{{book ref|mb6|19}} In 347 PC, the Set successfully developed a Harmonium bomb, although it would take another year for them to find a way to deliver it.{{book ref|mb7|33}}

The Set planned to blow up Elendel and then claim that Elendel had been the ones to develop the Harmonium bomb and accidentally blown themselves up. They’d use the resulting outrage to unite the Basin underneath them and then “recover” the Harmonium technology to use against the Malwish.{{book ref|mb7|46}}

When Wax came to Bilming to thwart their plans, Telsin intervened personally at the Sentinel of Truth broadsheet office to stall Wax.{{book ref|mb7|34}} Upon realizing her ploy, Wax got away.{{book ref|mb7|35}}

Telsin retreated to [[Independence Tower]], where she waited with the Hidden Guard (the Set’s elite personal army) and a decoy bomb to further delay Wax.{{book ref|mb7|61}} While confronting Wax, [[Harmony]] cut her [[Connection]] to Autonomy, leaving her weak and frail.{{book ref|mb6|66}} After her failure, Autonomy withdrew her power from Telsin, which led to her death.{{book ref|mb6|69}} Her body was found with the words “You have proven yourselves. For now.” scratched into her arm by her own hand.{{book ref|mb7|Epilogue|1}}

== Relationships ==
=== Wax ===
Telsin has hated Wax for decades. When they were young, she always thought he was judging her and thought himself too good for her.{{book ref|mb7|66}}  She thinks he’s a coward who ran from his responsibilities to “play hero” in the Roughs.{{book ref|mb7|27}}

===Autonomy===
Telsin is the one who convinced Autonomy that Scadrial had value and shouldn’t be destroyed completely.{{book ref|mb7|19}} Due to rivalry within the ranks of the Set, Telsin constantly has to prove that she’s the best choice to be Autonomy’s avatar.{{book ref|mb7|20}} Autonomy doesn’t think Telsin understands “true autonomy,” and her attempts to curry the shard’s favor are forced and insincere. However, were it not for Telsin’s efforts, she would have attacked the planet much earlier.{{book ref|mb7|55}} 

===Edwarn Ladrian===
Telsin outranks her uncle and he obeys her orders.{{book ref|mb6|26}} She’s always thought her uncle was an idiot.{{book ref|mb6|29}}
