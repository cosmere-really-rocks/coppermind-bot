'''Elendel University''' is a higher learning institution on post-[[Catacendre]] [[Scadrial]]. It is also referred to as the University of [[Elendel]].{{file ref|The Two Seasons broadsheet.jpg}} The university's [[High Imperial]] motto, “Wasing the always of wanting of knowing,” which is translated by [[Wayne]] to mean “the eternal desire of a hungry soul is knowledge,” is emblazoned on the gates. Both men and women may attend the university, but they are housed separately.{{book ref|mb4|4}}

The university has a large campus, with at least two schools, the school of Engineering and the school of Mathematics. There are also many social clubs on campus. These so called ''Thousand Eyes'' are considered to be a menace by many of the more serious students. Their pranks and initiation rites have created many headaches for serious students. Professor [[G. Geffenry]] even blamed them for the death of [[Jeddifry Tekiel]] in {{date|342|PC}}.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}}

== History ==
In {{date|335|PC}}, student Jeddifry Tekiel had a tragic accident and died. Most people seem to agree that it was an accident, but some people point fingers to the various social clubs at the university.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}} [[Marasi Colms]] attended the university and participated in the ladies rifle club, both of which gave her the skills to eventually become a constable.{{book ref|mb4|4}}

[[Wayne]] visited [[Allriandre]] at the university regularly, and the men at the gate knew to watch for him.{{book ref|mb4|5}} On the thirteenth of [[Scadrial#Calender|Marsh]] in {{date|342|PC}}, professor [[A. Hexaldweth]] discovered that one of her recent inventions, a device that uses electricity to compress and exchange refrigerating gases on the smallest scale yet seen, was gone. One of the windows in the otherwise locked laboratory had been smashed inwards. The professor blames [[Tarcsel Electric]] for the theft, stating that she believed they had stolen it after she had refused to sell it to them. Rather than blaming Tarcsel Electric, Professor G. Geffenry pointed a finger at the university clubs, stating that their pranks and initiation rites were a great headache to the university.{{file ref|CFM2 The House Record broadsheet.png|''The House Record'' broadsheet}} It is unclear what actually happened.

After experimenting some with [[trellium]] and [[harmonium]], [[Waxillium]] shared his findings with the university staff. They thanked him for the information, but they had already performed similar experiments and reached the same conclusions as Wax.{{book ref|mb7|13}}
